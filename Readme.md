# Retail Inventory & Supply Chain Decision Analytics

## Executive Summary:

Retail businesses need to balance product demand, inventory availability, and inventory movement to avoid stockouts and excess inventory. Using Python, SQL, and Power BI, I analyzed SKU-level sales, inventory, and movement data to identify inventory exposure, stockout risk, excess inventory, and demand patterns. The analysis provides visibility into inventory health and helps identify areas for better replenishment and inventory positioning.

### Business Problem:

Retail businesses need to maintain sufficient inventory to meet demand while avoiding excess stock. How can we identify inventory imbalances, stockout risk, and excess inventory across products and locations and use these insights to support better inventory decisions?

### Methodology:

1. Python analysis to clean, transform, validate, and prepare sales, inventory, and movement data.

2. SQL queries to create analytical datasets and analyze sales, inventory exposure, stockout risk, and inventory movements.

3. A Power BI dashboard to track sales trends, inventory health, inventory exposure, and movement patterns.

### Skills:

SQL: CTEs, Joins, Case, aggregate functions

Power BI: DAX, calculated columns, data modeling, data visualization

Python: Pandas, data cleaning, data transformation, exploratory data analysis

### Results & Business Insights:

The analysis identified inventory exposure across products and categories and provided visibility into stockout and excess inventory patterns. The Power BI dashboard enables users to analyze sales trends, inventory levels, inventory value, and movement activity across different products and locations.

The analysis also identified differences in inventory and demand patterns across product categories, helping highlight areas requiring closer inventory monitoring and replenishment attention.
